Spares SolidWorks add-in
========================

Staff install: close SolidWorks, right-click Install.cmd → Run as administrator.

Then open SolidWorks → Tools → Add-Ins → Spares.

Sign in with your oee360 staff email. Upload publishes the open assembly
as that staff user. Assign customer and site on the machine page.

Default URL in the pane: http://localhost:3000
Soft Launch: https://oee360.com   (app staff login, not Vercel team SSO)

Uninstall: Uninstall.cmd as administrator.
